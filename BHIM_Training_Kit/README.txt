BHIM AI Show - offline training kit

1. Keep this folder together. The slide buttons open demos/epN.html (relative links).
2. Open BHIM_AI_Training.pptx (36 slides, speaker notes = your speech with time stamps).
3. Demos: open demos/index.html in Edge or Chrome. No internet needed.
   Play = BHIM demo with voice. Back (or Left arrow) = revert a step. Your turn = you repeat the moves.
4. Indian English voice: pick a voice marked with a star. If none, add "English (India)" in
   Windows Settings > Time & language > Speech > Add voices, then reload the page.
5. Paste a screenshot of each news article into the dashed frame on every "Real incident" slide.
6. BHIM_Trainer_Speech_60min.docx = printed speech and 60-minute map.
All demo prices and scores are toy numbers. Incidents link to their original sources.
