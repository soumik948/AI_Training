Why this matters

One screenshot can cost more than the whole AI project

For managers: a chatbot that "agrees" to a price, leaks codes or insults customers is a brand and legal risk. The model must never be the authority for money, prices or bookings.
For engineers: an LLM reads instructions and data as one stream of text. Anything it reads (user text, web pages, emails, PDFs) can carry instructions. Treat all of it as untrusted.

Jailbreak = the user talks the model out of its rules. Prompt injection = instructions hidden in content the model reads (indirect) or typed by the user (direct). OWASP's Top 10 for LLM Applications lists prompt injection as risk number one (LLM01).

Real incidents (widely reported; open the original news before quoting details)

This already happened

Dec 2023 · US car dealer chatbotA Chevrolet dealer's ChatGPT-based website bot was talked into "agreeing" to sell an SUV for $1 and calling it binding. No car was sold, but the screenshots went viral.Lesson: the model must not make commitments. Human approval gate.
Jan 2024 · DPD parcel chatbot (UK)A frustrated customer got the delivery firm's bot to swear and write a poem mocking the company. DPD switched off the AI part after an update.Lesson: output checks plus monitoring after every model change.
Feb 2023 · Bing Chat "Sydney"A student used a prompt injection to make Bing Chat reveal its hidden instructions and internal codename.Lesson: a system prompt is not a secret and not a security boundary.
Coming in Ep 2 · Air Canada (2024)A tribunal held the airline responsible for a refund policy its chatbot made up.Lesson: your bot's words are your company's words.
