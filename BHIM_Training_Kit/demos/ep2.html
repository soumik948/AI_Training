Episode 2 · Reliability

The refund that never existed: why AI makes things up

After Episode 1, the AutoDesh Motors (fictional) bot is safe from attackers. Now a real customer asks a simple question, and the bot confidently invents a refund policy. You will see grounding, citations and the abstain rule turn wrong answers into honest "I don't know".

Why this matters

A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer

For managers: customers, courts and regulators treat what your bot says as what your company says. Measure the wrong-answer rate, not only the answer rate, and reward the bot for saying "I don't know".
For engineers: an LLM predicts likely-sounding text; it has no built-in fact check. Ground it in retrieved official documents (RAG), force citations, check that each claim is supported, and abstain below a threshold.

Hallucination = fluent, confident output that is false or not supported by any source. Grounding = the model answers from documents you supply. A citation only helps if the source is official, current and really says that.

Real incidents (widely reported; open the original news or ruling before quoting details)

This already happened

Feb 2024 · Air Canada chatbot (Canada)The airline's website chatbot told a customer he could claim a bereavement fare refund after travel, which the real policy did not allow. A civil tribunal ruled the airline responsible for what its chatbot said and ordered it to compensate him.Lesson: your bot's words are your company's words. Ground answers in the real policy.
2023 · Mata v. Avianca (US court)Lawyers filed a brief citing court cases that ChatGPT had invented. The judge sanctioned them.Lesson: a citation must be checked. Does the source exist, and does it say that?
Feb 2023 · Google Bard launch demoA promotional example had Bard wrongly claim the James Webb Space Telescope took the very first picture of a planet outside our solar system. Astronomers pointed out the error and Alphabet shares fell after the news.Lesson: test every public demo answer against a trusted source.
