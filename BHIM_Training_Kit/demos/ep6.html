Why this matters

Once data leaves, you cannot call it back

For managers: every prompt sent to an outside model is a data transfer. Ask: what personal data is in it, is it needed, was the customer told, and where is it stored and for how long? Fines are set per violation, not per company size.
For engineers: mask before you send, minimise before you mask, log what left, and choose a provider setting that does not train on or keep your data. Free text is the leakiest field: people type phone numbers into complaints.

DPDP = India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. PII = personally identifiable information. EU AI Act = the EU's risk-based AI law; it reaches AutoDesh only if it offers AI systems in the EU or their output is used there.

Real incident and the law (open the original sources before quoting details)

This already happened, and the rules now exist

2023 · Samsung staff and ChatGPTEmployees were widely reported to have pasted internal source code and meeting notes into ChatGPT. Samsung then restricted staff use of generative AI tools.Lesson: the leak is the paste, not a hack. Mask, minimise, set a policy. Verify details before quoting.
India · DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025Rules notified Nov 2025 and phased in: Data Protection Board at once; consent manager registration from Nov 2026; core duties (notice, consent, security safeguards, breach notice, children's data) from May 2027. Penalties up to ₹250 crore for failing security safeguards, ₹200 crore for breach notice or children's data failures.Sources: PIB note of 17 Nov 2025; AZB & Partners timeline. Sources differ by a day on the notification date (13 or 14 Nov 2025). Confirm in the Gazette.
EU · AI ActProhibited practices (for example emotion recognition at work, social scoring) apply since 2 Feb 2025. High-risk duties for uses like credit scoring and hiring now start 2 Dec 2027 after the Digital Omnibus delay.Source: EU AI Act Service Desk timeline; FPF analysis. Not legal advice.
